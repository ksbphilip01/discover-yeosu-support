Report something out of date

Discover Yeosu is a curated guide, and places in Yeosu open, move, and close. If an opening time is wrong, a restaurant has shut down, or a description needs correcting, email us and we will update it. Content in the app refreshes over the air, so corrections reach everyone without waiting for an app update.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no paywall. Open the app and everything is available.

Is the app free?

Yes. Discover Yeosu is free to use.

The app shows an error or will not load.

Discover Yeosu needs an internet connection — listings, maps, and translations are fetched live. Check your connection or Wi-Fi, then close and reopen the app. If it keeps failing, email us with your device model and what you were doing.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is optional. It is used only to show how far a place is from you and to work out directions. Decline it and the rest of the app still works — you simply will not see distances.

Why does the app ask for the microphone?

The microphone is used only by the translator, and only while you are using its Speak button for spoken input. It is not used anywhere else in the app.

How do I change my advertising privacy choices?

Open the Menu tab and tap "Ad privacy options" to reopen the consent form and change your answer at any time. You can also control app tracking in iOS under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

What personal data do you hold about me?

There is no account, so we hold no profile for you. Saved favourites stay on your own device. Full details are in the privacy policy linked below. If you have a question about your data or want to make a request, email us and we will respond.

Is the app available in Korean?

The interface is in English throughout — the app is built for visitors who do not read Korean. The built-in translator handles English and Korean in both directions.

About the app

Discover Yeosu is an English-language travel guide to Yeosu, on the southern coast of Korea. It covers attractions and islands, restaurants, cafes and bars, places to stay, ready-made routes, a map with directions, and practical tools for translation, transit, banking, and finding a hospital or pharmacy.
